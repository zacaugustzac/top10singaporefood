Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026

Top 10 Singapore Food ("we", "our", "the app") is a community voting platform for Singapore hawker food. This policy explains what data we collect when you use the app and how we use it.

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your email address from Google. We use this solely to identify your account so your votes are linked to you and cannot be duplicated.

We do not collect your name, profile photo, phone number, or any other personal information.

How we use it

Your email is used only to:

· Authenticate you when you sign in
· Associate your votes with your account to enforce the one-person-one-vote rule

We do not use your email for marketing, newsletters, or any other purpose.

Data storage

Authentication is handled by Supabase. Your email and session token are stored securely in their infrastructure. Vote data (which dishes or restaurants you voted for, and how many votes) is stored in our Supabase database. Your session token is stored in your browser's localStorage and is cleared when you sign out.

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or disclose your personal data to any third party, except as required by law.

Deleting your data

To have your account and votes removed, email us at zacaugustzac@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data". We will remove your data within 30 days.

Cookies

We do not use tracking cookies. The only browser storage we use is localStorage to keep you signed in between visits.

Contact

Questions about this policy? Email zacaugustzac@gmail.com.
